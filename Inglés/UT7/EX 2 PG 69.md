EX 3 PG 72



a) Why do tablets contribute to an easier and faster ordering service?

Customers can see pictures of the dishes and order directly on the tablet without waiting for a waiter. This also reduces mistakes and confusion in the kitchen.



b) Explain McDonald’s owner’s statement in your own words.

He means that the restaurant is designed for families to enjoy spending time together and have a different dining experience.



c) Do owners make more money with this new service? Provide examples.

Yes, they do. The tablets attract more families, and faster service allows restaurants to serve more customers each day. People also tend to order extra items, such as desserts or coffee.



d) How does the writer compare tablets with paper menus at the end of the text?

The writer points out that tablets cost more to buy and maintain than paper menus.



e) What is the most important disadvantage of this new service?

The main disadvantage is the loss of personal contact, because technology replaces human interaction during a social activity.
